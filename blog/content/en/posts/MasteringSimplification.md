# What is Simplification?

Simplification is a core practice in the era of Moore's Law. By focusing on simplification, it enhances efficiency, prioritizes critical issues, and uncovers higher-value solutions in complex and dynamic environments.

---

## Classic Simplification

### Focus on the Primary Contradiction
- **Core Idea**: Focus on solving "the most important task" and avoid distractions.
- **Common Problems with Multiple Objectives**:
  - Chasing easy tasks while ignoring truly valuable creations.
  - Fragmented efforts turn simple problems into complex ones.
  - Difficulty in delegation and implementation reduces team efficiency.
  - Indecisiveness traps people in cycles of hesitation and poor prioritization.

---

### Solve No Difficult Problems, Win First, Fight Later
- **Avoid Problems**:
  - Most problems are low-value or even negative-value.
  - Focus on high-value tasks and learn to ignore trivial problems.
- **Avoid Being Forced**:
  - Decisions and execution under pressure have a higher risk of failure.
  - Murphy’s Law amplifies complexity and creates vicious cycles.
- **Wait for Favorable Conditions**:
  - Watch for market extreme points (dimensional reduction or elevation) to seize clear opportunities.

---

### Eliminate Complexity and Perfectionism
- **Eliminate Complexity**: Apply the "KISS" principle (Keep It Simple, Stupid) to streamline tasks and remove redundancies.
- **Eliminate Perfectionism**: Perfectionism leads to failure; steady progress and continuous improvement are more important.

---

## Simplification in the Era of Moore's Law

### Extreme Chip Simplification
- Chip technology relies on large-scale repetition and replication, making simplification critical:
  - Reduce friction to improve production yield.
  - Control costs to enhance cost-effectiveness.

---

### Addition Equals Liability
- Each additional step magnifies the risks of costs and defects:
  - Simplify processes to reduce long-term complexity and financial burdens.

---

### Make Time Your Ally
- Simplification turns time into an advantage rather than a burden:
  - Strong personalities tend to "fill in gaps," adding complexity.
  - Focused minds "remove gaps" to reduce pressure and enhance concentration.

---

### Think Long-Term
- Use psychological distance to avoid short-term traps:
  - Abstract concepts like time and space to make better long-term decisions.

---

### Tenfold Resources, Half the Effort
- Concentrate resources on simplified tasks to achieve greater returns with less input:
  - Redefine tasks and align with simplification principles for high returns.

---

### Task Immune System
- Question whether a task is necessary before simplifying it:
  - Remove unreasonable elements before proceeding with optimization.

---

## Practices and Pitfalls

### Rebuilding Mental Strength
- **Recovery Ability**: Quickly bounce back from setbacks and maintain composure.
- **Growth Mindset**: View failures as learning opportunities and focus on consistent growth.

---

### Common Pitfalls
- **Grand Narratives**: Avoid using unrealistic "stories" to obscure the essence of problems.
- **Hindsight Bias**: Verifying problems is easier than solving them. Avoid wasting resources on "seemingly rational" directions.

---

## Advanced Simplification in the Era of Moore's Law

### The Future of Violent Computational Leverage
- AI and computational advancements offer new possibilities for simplification:
  - Leverage computation to unlock higher-dimensional insights, turning "search" into "generation."

---

### Rebuilding for Tenfold Returns
- Simplify core issues and redesign solutions with a tenfold perspective:
  - "Tenfold challenges are not necessarily tenfold harder." — Elon Musk

---

## Conclusion

Simplification is not just a technical philosophy but a core strategy for value creation. By simplifying, individuals and organizations can achieve maximum returns with minimal resources.

Chairman's evaluation of the philosophy of simplification: "Selfless yet decisive, focusing on major tasks, delegating effectively, perceiving clearly, calculating thoroughly, completing tasks, letting go when necessary."
